- Cultivate can triage issues assigned to the bot in GitHub Issues, Linear and Jira: `mimir cultivate setup --tracker github-issues|linear|jira` (repeatable) installs the optional `tracker-poller` skill, writes the `tracker:` section of `worklink.yaml`, stores the Linear and Jira credentials in `compose.env` (`--linear-api-key-file`, `--jira-email-file`, `--jira-api-token-file` and their `-stdin` forms) and remembers the selection on later runs. Only threads whose latest activity comes from a trusted person reach the agent.
